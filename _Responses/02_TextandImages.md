# Digital Text and Image

## Description

Readings and Games for this assignment can be found [here](https://scrippscollege.box.com/s/3je1kmbfik4jxdl2dhqeoajkj3sh1owr) (Check the class Discord for password.)

**Don’t wait until the weekend before discussion to begin engaging with the sources for our response. During the weeks between discussions,  move through the sources slowly**

**This response is due by 5pm the day before our discussion.**

For this response, do the following:

1. Read [Electronic Literature: What is it?](https://eliterature.org/pad/elp.html) (2007) by N. Katherine Hayles
2. Play 30 min of [Colossal Cave Adventure](https://rickadams.org/adventure/) (1976-1977) Will Crowther and Don Woods
3. Engage with [*i feel so much shame*](https://ifeelsomuchsha.me/) by Jackie Liu (2024) [more information](https://jackieis.online/projects/i-feel-so-much-shame)
4. Engage for 30 min with [*My Boyfriend Came Back From the War*](http://www.teleportacia.org/war/) (1996) by Olia Lialina
5. Play 2 hours of [*Kentucky Route Zero*](https://scrippscollege.box.com/s/j0drr44ir0g9sjpl0x5l71tqosxfsdye) (2020) by Card Board Computer (If windows blocks the file press More Info > Run Anyway) (Please let me know if you have any issues launching the game).

## Submit

In the Discord channel for Response 2 and Canvas, post either a 500 word or 3 min video response to the above media. Guiding questions:

- How do these works use the affordances of code, digital media, and interactivity to create a unique relation between text, image, and the viewer? How do works provide for different experiences with text and image beyond printed media?
