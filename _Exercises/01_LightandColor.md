# Light and Color

## Part 1: Exhibition

1. Read this article in our class [library](https://scrippscollege.box.com/s/3je1kmbfik4jxdl2dhqeoajkj3sh1owr) about the work of Mavis Pusey.
2. Read [Alt Text as Poetry](https://alt-text-as-poetry.net/#about). 
3. Attend the Mavis Pusey Restless exhibition at the [Benton Museum of Art](https://www.pomona.edu/museum/visit) at Pomona. Museum Hours (Thursday 11 am–8 pm  and Friday–Sunday 11 am–6 pm) 
4. Choose one of the art works in the exhibition and write an alt text description of the work.

## Part 2: Composition

1. Make your own composition using p5.js! Explore the relationship between different shapes and colors. In what ways do they effect the feel and mood of the composition? How does Pusey use of color and shape? What approach do you want to take? Notice how Pusey pulled from her life around her to create her compositions.
2. Considering accessibility, use the `describe()` function ([Reference](https://p5js.org/reference/p5/describe/)) in your p5.js sketch to create a screen reader-accessible description of your composition. Make sure to use the `describe()` function within `setup()`. Notice that the text of your `describe()` function needs to be between `' '` marks.  Consider the words you are using to describe your art work and use Alt Text as Poetry for reference.
 
Feel free to experiment with:

- Alpha
- `ellipse()`[(Reference)](https://p5js.org/reference/p5/ellipse/)
- `quad()` [(Reference)](https://p5js.org/reference/p5/quad/)
- `triangle()`[(Reference)](https://p5js.org/reference/p5/triangle/).
- `blendMode()`[(Reference)](https://p5js.org/reference/p5/blendMode/)
