# p5 Poetics

## Description

Using text, font and arrays, create a p5 sketch that makes an experimental piece of poetry. This piece of poetry can take any form, perhaps words are hidden and then revealed with the mouse, perhaps words or sentences move around the canvas, or perhaps lines of a poem are randomly generated. Consider the font, colors, shape, and scale of your text.
