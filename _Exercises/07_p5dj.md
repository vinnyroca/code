# p5.dj

## Description

Make a p5.js sketch that has a series of buttons and optionally sliders and knobs that play a variety of sound files or produce sounds using p5.Oscillator or p5.MonoSynth. You can take in input using mouse clicks or key buttons.

- Submit p5 share link to Canvas and exercise-submission channel on Discord
